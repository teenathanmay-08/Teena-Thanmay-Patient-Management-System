About Teena Thanmay

Teena Thanmay is a demonstration clinic interface designed to help staff maintain patient information in a structured database. It provides registration, search, editing, and deletion of patient records.

System features

  • Centralized patient records
  • Search by ID, name, or email
  • Patient registration and updates
  • Responsive design
